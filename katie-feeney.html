01 · Early Life

Early Life & Family

Katherine Grace Feeney was born on August 16, 2002, in Olney, Maryland, a suburb north of Washington, D.C. She is the only daughter in her family and grew up with two older brothers, Peter and Michael. Her parents keep a low profile, and their names are not public.

Katie started dancing at the age of four and competed for several years. She trained with dancers connected to the Ziegler sisters and has been linked to the Abby Lee Dance Company. As a kid she was also an ambassador for Dance Hope Cure, a group that raises awareness about childhood cancer.

A Maryland dancer turned a phone camera into a sports media career.

At Sherwood High School she was student government treasurer, ran track and was on the dance team. She graduated in 2021 and enrolled at Penn State University to study broadcast journalism, with a goal of hosting her own show or podcast. She is also an animal lover and has two dogs, Maggie and Tess.

02 · The Rise

From Musical.ly to 7.6 Million on TikTok

Katie posted her first videos in 2015 when TikTok was still called Musical.ly. She kept creating through high school, and by the start of the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 she had about 2 million followers. During lockdown she turned to sea monkey growth videos, unboxings, DIY and comedy, which took her audience much higher.

Charli D'Amelio once dueted one of her dance videos set to a Madcon track, and the video passed 71 million views. Katie was also among the early creators to earn a verified badge. Today her main account, @katiefeeneyy, shows about 7.6 million followers and roughly 654.8 million likes.

Snapchat Record

Over $1 million from Snapchat Spotlight in six weeks.

In February 2021 she made national news when she earned more than one million dollars from Snapchat Spotlight in about six weeks, a first for the platform at the time. She was 18 and still in high school.

04 · Personal Life

Jack Hurley & Relationship History

Katie's first long relationship was with Sean Yamada, a childhood friend who became her high school sweetheart. He appeared in her videos and was her senior prom date. Reports say they split in early 2022, and she addressed it in a YouTube Shorts Q&A that February.

In May 2022 she shared a photo captioned "Hey, ACC champ," and the man turned out to be Jack Hurley, then a Virginia Tech outfielder. A month later she posted another photo of them together, and the couple became a regular presence on each other's feeds.

Jack Hurley was born on March 13, 2002, in State College, Pennsylvania. At Virginia Tech he hit .375 with 14 home runs and 55 RBIs in his sophomore season, and the Arizona Diamondbacks drafted him in 2023. The two have appeared together on a college baseball podcast and in prank and couple videos.

07 · In Her Words

Interviews & What She Has Said

Speaking to The Hollywood Reporter, Katie explained that creators have to keep reinventing themselves because what worked before may stop working. She told Onward State in 2025 that capturing viral moments has become second nature, and that she watches for scenes on and off the field her audience will enjoy.

She has also said she stays grounded even with an unusual career, and that family and friends matter most to her. In 2026 she was a keynote speaker at Her Conference in New York City, talking to more than 1,000 young women about her path.
