01 · Early Life

Early Life & Family

Haley Bea O'Brien was born on August 6, 1992, in Excelsior, Minnesota, a lake town west of Minneapolis. She is the middle of three children, with an older sister and a younger brother, and both of her parents are mechanical engineers.

She went to Minnetonka High School, and the science-heavy household shaped her path. She later described herself as a nerd first and a model second.

A biology student from Minnesota turned a pageant crown into a global creator career.

At St. Cloud State University she graduated summa cum laude with a Bachelor of Science in medical biology and psychology, plus a minor in chemistry. She was a senior in college when she won her biggest pageant title.

She has often used her science background in interviews and posts, and in 2022 she spoke publicly about supporting women in STEM.

02 · Pageants

Pageant Years

Haley began competing at 16. She placed third runner-up at Miss Minnesota Teen USA 2009, came back the next year and was crowned Miss Minnesota Teen USA 2010. She then competed at Miss Teen USA 2010 in the Bahamas but did not place.

In 2012 she won Miss Collegiate America. In late 2013 she was crowned Miss Minnesota USA 2014 and took the Top 20 at Miss USA 2014, where Nia Sanchez of Nevada took the crown. After her reign she passed the title to Jessica Scheu and retired from pageants. She later judged the Miss USA 2021 competition.

04 · Personal Life

Marriage & Personal Life

Haley married NFL offensive lineman Matt Kalil, the fourth overall pick in the 2012 draft, on July 8, 2015, in Kauai, Hawaii. They renewed their vows a year later. She filed for divorce on May 4, 2022, and they have no children.

In 2022 she also went public about a health battle. She was diagnosed with endometriosis, urethritis and ovarian cysts and had surgery, and she said she had been in a great deal of pain.

By August 2026 she was in a new relationship of about three months. She has posted him on Instagram, joking that he looks like Jacob Elordi, but has not named him.

05 · Public Attention

Controversies & Lawsuit

The first big controversy came in May 2024. On Met Gala day she posted a TikTok in a doll-like look, lip-syncing to a Marie Antoinette line. Many viewers called it tone-deaf while protests were happening over the Gaza conflict, and it became a trigger for the Blockout 2024 campaign. She posted a nine-minute apology saying she never meant to offend anyone.

The second came in November 2025 on a Twitch livestream with Marlon Garcia. She talked about why her marriage ended and described physical incompatibility, without naming Matt Kalil. He sued in January 2026 for public disclosure of private facts and unjust enrichment. She moved to dismiss on January 23.

Latest Update

The lawsuit was dismissed on September 17, 2026.

Chief U.S. District Judge Patrick Schiltz threw out the case with prejudice, ruling that the topic was of legitimate public concern. Haley said she felt relief and hoped it encourages women to speak openly. Matt Kalil's lawyer has said they plan to appeal, so the story may not be over.

Outside these two episodes, no other verified legal issues were found as of September 2026.

06 · Interviews

Interviews & What She Has Said

In a 2022 Untitled Magazine interview she talked about balancing modeling with science and encouraging women in STEM. In March 2023 she told Sports Illustrated Swimsuit that bad experiences were why she would stop working with modeling agencies.

Before the Let's Marry Harry reunion, she told Us Weekly she was thrilled because she had been watching the show closely and had plenty of questions for the cast. After Forbes named her a 2026 Top Creator, she wrote on Instagram that she was beyond grateful and called the job a dream.

After the court win in September 2026 she said she was relieved and hoped women feel freer to speak about their own experiences.
