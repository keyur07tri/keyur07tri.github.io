01 · Early Life

Early Life & Family

Kayla Itsines was born on May 21, 1991, in Adelaide, South Australia, and is of Greek-Australian heritage. Her parents, Anna and Jim Itsines, are both teachers, and she has a younger sister, Leah Itsines. As a child she played sports such as soccer, netball and basketball, which is where her interest in fitness began.

She first planned to become a beauty therapist, but she enjoyed how training made her feel and switched direction. She graduated from the Australian Institute of Fitness as a Master Trainer at 18 and has said she has been training women since 2008, starting at a women's gym in Adelaide.

She did not start as an influencer. She started as a trainer working with women one at a time.

Kayla still keeps her family close. Press interviews describe her living near her parents and grandparents in Adelaide, who help with her children while she runs her business.

02 · Career

Career Timeline

Kayla's career grew from one-on-one sessions to a global digital brand. In March 2013 she and then-fiance Tobi Pearce founded the Bikini Body Training Company, with Kayla as director and Tobi as CEO. They sold her workouts as Bikini Body Guide (BBG) ebooks, and her client transformation photos on Instagram helped the program spread quickly around the world.

In 2015 she launched the free Kayla Itsines Bootcamp World Tour and was featured in an Apple Watch advertisement. The same year the pair launched the Sweat with Kayla app, which was later renamed simply Sweat and added other trainers such as Kelsey Wells and Sjana Elise. Wikipedia notes that in 2016 the app generated more revenue than any other fitness app.

Her first print book, The Bikini Body 28-Day Eating and Lifestyle Guide, was published by Pan Macmillan. In 2021 Sweat was sold to the US company iFIT, and in late 2023 Kayla and Tobi bought it back. In March 2026 she announced she was the sole owner of Sweat.

06 · BBG & Sweat

Bikini Body Guide (BBG) & Sweat App

BBG, short for Bikini Body Guide, is the program that made Kayla famous. Its workouts are short, usually 28 minutes, and are built from circuits that you repeat, combined with low-intensity cardio and HIIT days. The 28 Day Healthy Eating and Lifestyle Guide was designed to go with it. People often search for "BBG 2.0", "BBG week 1 day 1" and "bikini body guide before and after" to find the structure and results. The brand has also been reported to have renamed the guides in recent years, so check the official Sweat site for the current names.

The Sweat app is where her programs now live. It offers workouts, meal plans and programs from several trainers, including strength, pregnancy and postpartum tracks. Kayla's own programs include High Intensity with Kayla (the modern take on BBG) and a post-pregnancy program.

App price, free trials and discounts such as Black Friday offers change often, and they differ by country. Check the official Sweat website or the app store listing for the current Sweat app cost instead of trusting old articles.

07 · Workout Plan

Kayla Itsines Workout Plan

The Kayla Itsines workout plan is known for short, intense sessions. A typical BBG-style week mixes about three resistance sessions with cardio days and a rest day. Below is an example layout inspired by the public structure of BBG. It is not the exact official program, so use the Sweat app for the real plan.

MondayResistance circuit (about 28 minutes), full body
TuesdayLow-intensity cardio such as a brisk walk, 30 to 40 minutes
WednesdayResistance circuit, legs and glutes focus
ThursdayHIIT or interval session, short and intense
FridayResistance circuit, arms and abs focus
SaturdayLow-intensity cardio or a long walk
SundayRest and stretching

The resistance sessions are usually done as two circuits, each repeated for a set time, with moves such as squats, lunges, push-ups, tricep dips and ab exercises. Many people search for her "ab workout", "arm workout", "back workout" and "full body workout" for this reason, and she has also posted 7, 10, 15 and 30 minute sessions on YouTube.

  • Warm up for 5 to 10 minutes before every session.
  • Keep rest short, but stop if your form falls apart.
  • Progress by adding reps, weight or a slower tempo, not by rushing.
  • Take at least one full rest day a week.

08 · BBG Week 1

Kayla Itsines Week 1, Day 1 & Weekly Layout

Fans often search "Kayla Itsines week 1 day 1", "week 1 Monday", "week 1 Wednesday" and "week 2 Friday". The official program is paid and updated, so we do not copy it. In general terms, week 1 starts gently with bodyweight or light-weight circuits on resistance days, easy cardio in between, and plenty of time to learn the moves. Later weeks, such as weeks 2, 3, 5, 6 and 8, add more volume and intensity.

A sensible approach for beginners is to treat week 1 as a trial: learn the exercises with light or no weights, keep a record of what you did, and add effort gradually. If you have pain, dizziness or a health condition, stop and speak to a doctor or qualified trainer.

10 · Tips

Kayla Itsines Workout Tips & Pre-Workout

Kayla has repeatedly said that consistency matters more than perfection. These tips reflect the themes she talks about most.

  • Start where you are. Pick a program that fits your level and build up slowly.
  • Be consistent. Three or four sessions a week done every week beat a perfect month followed by a break.
  • Focus on form. Good technique protects joints and makes each rep count.
  • Mix strength and cardio. Resistance training and low-intensity cardio work well together.
  • Fuel and rest. Eat enough, drink water and sleep 7 to 9 hours.
  • Do not compare. Progress photos and numbers are personal, and bodies change at different speeds.
  • Pre-workout. A light carbohydrate snack such as a banana 30 to 60 minutes before training, plus water, is enough for most people. Caffeine or supplement products are optional, so read labels and ask a doctor if unsure.
11 · Pregnancy

Pregnancy, Postpartum & Motherhood

Kayla has shared both of her pregnancies and her return to training. Daughter Arna was born in 2019, and son Jax was born in January 2023. After her first baby she built a post-pregnancy program, and people searching "Kayla Itsines pregnant" or "pregnant again" are usually looking for these stories and workouts.

Her message for new mothers is to go slowly and follow medical advice. Anyone who is pregnant or recently gave birth should get clearance from a doctor or physiotherapist before starting a program, and use programs designed for pregnancy or postpartum rather than copying a general routine.
