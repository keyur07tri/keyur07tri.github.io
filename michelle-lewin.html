01 · Early Life

Early Life & Family

Michelle Lewin was born on February 25, 1986, in Maracay, a city in north-central Venezuela, and grew up there. Several profiles describe a modest upbringing, and she has built her image around hard work rather than family connections. Details about her parents and siblings are rarely shared, so this page does not guess at names.

Before fitness became her career, she worked as a model and approached fashion agencies. In an interview with Iron Man magazine she explained that fitness modeling came later, after her body changed through training and fitness magazines began to notice her. A 2015 Spanish report said she weighed around 67 kg in 2009 and ate without restriction, which is the starting point behind the "before" photos fans often look for.

She did not arrive in the gym as an athlete. The discipline came first, and the career followed.

Her school and college history is not publicly documented, so we have left it out rather than repeat unverified claims. What is clear is that her real education came in the gym, first with her husband and coach Jimmy Lewin and later through years of bikini competition.

02 · Career

Career Timeline

Lewin began in fashion and glamour modeling in Venezuela, including a January 2012 appearance in the Venezuelan edition of Playboy, which gave her a national profile. The same year she moved to South Florida, where she settled in Miami and committed to training under Jimmy. Soon fitness magazines and supplement brands began booking her, and she appeared on covers of titles such as Muscle & Fitness, Oxygen and Inside Fitness.

She entered NPC bikini competitions in 2013 and turned pro with the IFBB in 2014, finishing third at two professional shows that year, the Europa SuperShow in Dallas and the Legends Pro Bikini Classic. Fitness databases list around eight contest results in total and mark her as retired from competing. She also took part as a contestant in the Spanish-language competition show Exatlón USA.

Her real breakthrough was online. In 2017 Forbes named her among the top fitness influencers and reported that partnerships with health and workout brands could pay her as much as $10,000 for a single Instagram post. She then turned that audience into products: programs and trainer work on the Fitplan app, a 2018 book, her One0One clothing and supplement brand, and most recently the PulseFit training app.

03 · Personal Life

Personal Life & Social Media

Michelle Lewin is married to Jimmy Lewin, a Swedish-born personal trainer and fitness model. They met in 2008, married in 2010, and have worked side by side ever since, with Jimmy acting as her coach and co-owner of One0One.

In September 2020 she told followers she would step away from social media because Jimmy was in critical condition. His diagnosis included acute liver failure, kidney failure, and heart failure linked to an aortic aneurysm and dissection, and she mentioned that he had tested positive for Covid-19 several weeks earlier. Within days she reported he was out of intensive care, then off dialysis, and in March 2021 she asked fans for support as he faced open-heart surgery. The episode is one of the most widely followed personal moments of her career.

In August 2022 she announced she was pregnant, and the couple welcomed a son, Landrew, around the end of that year. Her pregnancy and postpartum training videos became a major part of her content afterward.

Online, she is best known for her Instagram account (@michelle_lewin, roughly 15 million followers), her TikTok, and a YouTube channel with several hundred thousand subscribers. She posts in both English and Spanish, mixing workouts, motivation, product launches and family moments.

06 · Workout Plan

Michelle Lewin Workout Plan

Michelle Lewin's training mixes weights with plenty of cardio. Coverage of her routine describes a body-part split of about five days a week, with a strong focus on legs and glutes, and she has said she likes to sweat on the StairMaster, in spinning classes or while rollerblading around Miami Beach. The weekly layout below is built from the sessions that have been reported, with sensible fill-ins where the details were not shared.

MondayBack and biceps: chin-ups or assisted pull-ups, rows, lat pulldowns, curls
TuesdayHamstrings and calves: lying and seated leg curls, Romanian deadlifts, calf raises
WednesdayShoulders and triceps: presses, side lateral raises, skull crushers, triceps extensions
ThursdayQuads and glutes: squats, lunges, leg press, hip thrusts
FridayGlutes plus cardio: sumo squats, kickbacks, StairMaster or incline walking
SaturdayAbs: hanging knee raises, crunches, leg raises, ball crunches
SundayRest or light activity: walking, stretching, easy spinning

Monday to Wednesday and Saturday follow sessions that have been published; Thursday and Friday are typical glute-focused fill-ins and may differ from her own schedule. Use roughly 3 to 4 sets of 10 to 15 reps per exercise as a starting point.

Moves she has often recommended include sprints, lunges and side lateral raises. For a quick option she has shared a short legs-and-glutes circuit built around lunges, jump moves and sumo squats, and she is known for training with a waist belt, which she says she picked up from fellow competitors.

  • Warm up for 5 to 10 minutes before lifting.
  • Add weight or reps gradually so each week is slightly harder than the last.
  • Train 3 to 5 days a week and keep at least one full rest day.
  • Finish with cardio you enjoy, since consistency matters more than the machine.

07 · Diet Plan

Michelle Lewin Diet Plan

Lewin describes the diet as the most important part of building a physique. The style associated with her is simple and clean: lean protein at most meals, lots of vegetables, controlled carbohydrates and little processed food. She has also said she leans toward plant-based eating at times. Her 2018 book The Hot Body Diet, written with health coach Dr. Samar Yorde and published by Penguin, packages this into a 28-day plan with meals, recipes and exercise tips.

BreakfastProtein pancakes (oats, egg whites, banana, protein powder) or eggs with vegetables
SnackGreek yogurt with berries, or a piece of fruit with a handful of nuts
LunchGrilled chicken, fish or tofu with rice or sweet potato and a large salad
Pre-workoutBanana or a small carb snack with water or black coffee
DinnerLean protein with steamed or roasted vegetables and a small carb portion
HabitsPlenty of water, planned meals and a weekly cheat meal in moderation

This is an example day, not her exact menu, and portions depend on your size, goals and activity. A registered dietitian can set targets that fit you better than any celebrity plan.

08 · Quick Food Plan

Michelle Lewin Quick Food Plan

A quick food plan in the Michelle Lewin style is about speed, protein and planning ahead. Busy days are easier when the base ingredients are cooked once and mixed in different ways during the week.

  • Protein pancakes: blend oats, egg whites, banana and protein powder, then cook for 5 minutes.
  • Egg-white veggie scramble with spinach, peppers and a slice of whole-grain toast.
  • Greek yogurt bowl with berries, chia seeds and a drizzle of honey.
  • Chicken and rice bowl using pre-cooked chicken, microwave rice and frozen vegetables.
  • Tuna or chicken wrap with lettuce, tomato and mustard instead of heavy sauces.
  • Overnight oats with protein powder, ready in the fridge for the morning.
  • A protein smoothie with fruit and spinach for days with no time to cook.

Cook proteins and grains in bulk on one day, portion them into containers and keep fruit, yogurt and nuts at hand for snacks. That habit does more for results than any single recipe.

09 · Fat Burner & Supplements

Michelle Lewin Fat Burner & Supplements

People often search for the fat burner Michelle Lewin uses. No single product is confirmed as the reason for her physique, and her results are usually credited to years of training, diet and consistency. Her brand One0One does sell supplements alongside clothing and accessories, so the official shop is the place to see what is currently offered.

Fat burners are usually stimulant-based, with caffeine as the main ingredient, and they cannot replace a calorie balance, protein, resistance training and sleep. Read the label, avoid mixing several stimulant products, and ask a doctor first if you have heart, blood pressure, anxiety or sleep problems, or if you are pregnant or under 18. Treat before-and-after claims for any burner with caution.

10 · Fitplan & PulseFit

Fitplan, PulseFit & Lewin Fitness Platform

Michelle Lewin first reached a wide training audience as a trainer on the Fitplan app, where she announced she would be a trainer for the platform's users. Her programs there ran in weekly blocks of workouts and nutrition guidance. More recently she announced the PulseFit app, which she said was years in the making and would launch on January 1, 2026 with a new training experience.

Fans sometimes call this wider setup the Lewin Fitness platform, meaning the training plans, videos and meal ideas that she and Jimmy have shared across apps, Instagram and YouTube. Pricing and features change often, so check the official app store listing or her Instagram links before you subscribe.

11 · One0One Shop

One0One (One on One), Shop & Fajas

Fans search for the brand in many ways, including One on One, One O One, One0One and One 0 One, but they all point to the same company. One0One is run by Michelle and Jimmy Lewin and sells workout clothing, supplements, fitness gear, accessories and swimsuits. It also has a physical store in Miami, Florida, and its online shop is at one0one.com.

The brand is also tied to waist belts, called fajas in Spanish. Lewin has said she did not believe in them until she saw fellow competitors using one to shape the waist and then made it part of her routine. A waist belt is a styling and posture tool, not a fat-loss tool, and it should never feel painful or restrict breathing. Avoid wearing one through intense exercise unless the product is designed for it.
