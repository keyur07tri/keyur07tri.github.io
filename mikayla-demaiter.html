01 · Early Life

Early Life & Hockey Goalie Years

Mikayla Demaiter was born on May 14, 2000, in Chatham, Ontario, a small city in southwestern Canada. She grew up in a sports-loving environment, started playing ice hockey young and chose the goaltender position, one of the most demanding spots on the ice. Her parents and siblings keep a low profile, and their names are not public.

She played goalie for the Bluewater Hawks in the Provincial Women's Hockey League. In the 2018-19 season she appeared in 20 games with a goals-against average of 2.25, according to profile data. She later attended Western University.

A goalie who learned to handle pressure between the pipes, then built an audience of millions.

In 2019, at age 19, she retired from competitive hockey after an injury, which reports say was to her knee. That decision opened the door to her second career.

02 · The Rise

From the Ice to Instagram

Mikayla began sharing modeling photos on Instagram in January 2017, while she was still playing. After leaving hockey she committed to modeling and content creation full time. Her hockey background became part of her brand, and tabloids dubbed her the "world's hottest hockey player," a label that helped her photos travel across the internet.

She signed with Verge Talent Management in 2022, became a SportX influencer and ambassador, and worked with brands including Manscaped and Urban Planet. Her feed is built around fashion, fitness, swimwear and travel, with trips to the United States, Greece, Italy and the Caribbean. Instagram now shows about 3.1 million followers.
