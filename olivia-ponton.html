01 · Early Life

Early Life & Family

Olivia Marie Ponton was born on May 30, 2002, in Naples, Florida. Her mother, Diane, works for an education non-profit and her father, Richard, is a teacher. She grew up on Florida's Gulf Coast and stayed close to sports from a young age.

At Naples High School she took part in cheerleading and track and field. During her senior year she visited New York City on spring break and signed with Wilhelmina Models, an early sign of the career ahead.

A pandemic hobby of posting workout videos became a modeling and media career.

She graduated in 2020, just as lockdowns kept everyone at home. With little else to do, she started posting to TikTok, and the audience grew fast.

Ponton has kept most of her family life private. What is public is that she grew up in a home with two education-focused parents, and she has often described Florida as the place that shaped her outdoorsy, active side.

She signed with her first agency, Wilhelmina Models, at 17. According to an Elite Daily profile, the pandemic hit only about two weeks later, which is why she shifted her focus to creating content online. She chose not to enroll in college so she could focus on her career.

02 · Career

Career Timeline

Ponton first gained attention on TikTok, then signed with IMG Models. Since then she has appeared in campaigns for Coach, Skims, Calvin Klein and Hugo Boss, and she has also been linked to Victoria's Secret work.

Her Sports Illustrated Swimsuit shoots, a cameo in the 2024 film It Ends with Us and a growing media presence show a career built well beyond one platform.

Her Hype House stint in 2020 put her in front of a much bigger audience. She lived in the Los Angeles creator house for around three months. Later, she has said Sports Illustrated Swimsuit first noticed her through TikTok when she was 18, and that she was the first creator from that world they worked with.

Beyond Coach, Skims, Calvin Klein and Hugo Boss, her client list includes Burberry, Tory Burch, Ralph Lauren, Tommy Hilfiger, Maybelline and Victoria's Secret (including PINK). She has also sat front row at New York Fashion Week and attended Vogue World.

In October 2025 she launched Booked & Busy on YouTube, a book podcast that began with a deep dive into Fourth Wing by Rebecca Yarros. Author Alex Aster was among her early guests. For Season 2 on June 1, 2026, the show became Booked, Blonde & Busy and opened up to friends, fans and celebrities as well as authors.

03 · Personal Life

Personal Life & Social Media

Ponton came out as bisexual in a June 2021 Teen Vogue interview and later said she is pansexual. She has credited JoJo Siwa as an inspiration for speaking publicly. Her earlier relationship with soccer player Kaila Novak ended a few months after it became public.

Online, she is most active on TikTok (@iamoliviaponton, around 7.5 million followers) and Instagram (@olivia.ponton, roughly 2.8 to 3 million followers). She also runs a YouTube channel and hosts the podcast Booked, Blonde & Busy, where she talks about books, fashion and culture.

Ponton has spoken openly about mental health and is a vocal supporter of the LGBTQ+ community, using her platform to encourage people to live authentically. Reading became a big part of her life around 2021, when she picked up fiction as a way to unwind from the pace of early influencer fame.

She is based in New York City, and her feed mixes travel, fashion weeks, workouts, get-ready-with-me videos and book recommendations.

04 · Public Attention

Rumors & Public Attention

Ponton's name reached a wider audience in December 2024, when she reported a burglary at NFL quarterback Joe Burrow's home in Ohio. The police report listed her as one of his employees, and dating speculation followed.

Ponton has used her podcast to push back on how the public sees her, describing it as a way to challenge the blonde stereotype.
