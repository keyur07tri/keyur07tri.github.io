01 · Early Life

Early Life & Family

Jelena Noura Hadid was born on April 23, 1995, in Los Angeles, California. Her father, Mohamed Hadid, is a Palestinian-American real estate developer, and her mother, Yolanda Hadid, is a Dutch former model who later starred in The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills. The nickname Gigi came from her mother and became her name at school.

She is the oldest of three siblings, with a younger sister, Bella, and a younger brother, Anwar, who are both models. Through her father she also has older half-sisters, Marielle and Alana, and a younger half-sister, Aydan Nix, whom the family publicly welcomed in May 2025.

A Malibu volleyball captain who grew up to open runway shows in Paris and Milan.

The family lived on a ranch in Santa Barbara for about ten years before moving to Beverly Hills. Gigi loved horseback riding and volleyball, captained the team at Malibu High School and graduated in 2013. Her modeling story actually began at age two, when Guess co-founder Paul Marciano cast her in Baby Guess ads. She then paused to focus on school.

02 · Heritage

Is Gigi Hadid a Muslim? Religion & Heritage

This is one of the most searched questions about her. Her father is a Palestinian Muslim and her mother comes from a Dutch Christian background, so Gigi grew up with both influences. Reports say she and her siblings were raised with a strong Muslim influence, and Bella has said their father always prayed with the family.

Gigi herself has been more private. In a Vogue interview about raising her daughter, she said she felt free to learn about every religion as a child and hopes Khai can take meaningful pieces from different traditions. She has not publicly described her own practice in detail, so a simple yes or no label would go beyond what she has said.

03 · Career

Modeling Career, Vogue & Milestones

Gigi signed with IMG Models in 2013 and made her New York Fashion Week debut in 2014. She quickly moved into major campaigns and covers, and Wikipedia says she has appeared in international editions of Vogue at least 50 times, including five American Vogue covers. She also became a face of Maybelline.

04 · Fashion

Runway, Met Gala & Fashion Moments

Fashion is where Gigi still shines brightest. On May 4, 2026, she attended the "Costume Art" Met Gala in a custom Miu Miu look. She told Vogue she thought of the outfit as her body being the canvas, and that she calms herself on the carpet by thinking of her daughter first and then her partner.

In August she landed the cover of Vogue France's September 2026 issue, photographed in Paris by Willy Vanderperre in a black lace Saint Laurent gown. On September 22 she opened Vogue World 2026: Milano in Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II wearing a Valentino gold brocade gown made from recycled Venetian quilts, which took 1,680 hours of handwork. She came back later in a custom white Gucci dress.

She also opened the equestrian segment of Vogue World 2024 on horseback in Hermès. In July 2026 Maxim and other outlets reported she will return to the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, which will be staged in Los Angeles this year.

05 · Business

Guest in Residence Cashmere, Amazon & Swimwear

Guest in Residence. Gigi launched her cashmere brand on September 7, 2022, inspired by borrowing her parents' cashmere sweaters and by wanting more time at home with Khai. The label focuses on 100% cashmere knitwear as "future heirlooms." It opened a NoHo flagship on Bond Street in October 2023, a Los Angeles store in late 2024, and in 2026 leased a 3,550 sq ft Upper East Side space on Madison Avenue. Its Spring/Summer 2026 collection dropped in February.

Gigi Hadid Amazon. We found no verified official Gigi Hadid Amazon storefront. What does exist is her Reebok x Gigi Hadid capsule, which has been listed on Amazon's Reebok store, while her cashmere line sells through its own site and boutiques.

Gigi Hadid bikinis. Swimwear has been part of her career for years. She has fronted swimwear campaigns, including a Frankies Bikinis shoot, and is listed as a Sports Illustrated Swimsuit model. In 2026 she styled bikini tops into her Guest in Residence spring looks.

06 · Relationships

Zayn Malik, Leonardo DiCaprio & Bradley Cooper

Zayn Malik. Gigi dated former One Direction singer Zayn Malik from 2015 until October 2021, with breaks along the way. They welcomed daughter Khai in September 2020 and now co-parent.

Leonardo DiCaprio. She casually dated Leonardo DiCaprio from about September 2022. Reports described a split in early 2023, a reunion around the Oscars and a final end in September 2023, with sources citing lifestyle differences and her priority on being a mom. Neither described it as a serious long-term romance.

Bradley Cooper. They were first linked in October 2023 and, according to Gigi, met at a child's birthday party. She told Vogue in March 2025 that the relationship is "very romantic and happy." Cooper attended her 30th birthday in April 2025, and they went Instagram official soon after. At the 2026 Met Gala they shared an affectionate moment inside, though Cooper skipped the carpet. People sources have said they are serious but not rushing an engagement, and there is no confirmed engagement.

07 · Sisters & Friends

Bella Hadid, Kendall Jenner & Close Circle

Gigi and Bella are one of fashion's most famous sister pairs. Bella, born October 9, 1996, was named Model of the Year by the British Fashion Council in 2022, six years after Gigi won International Model of the Year. Gigi has said they have very different looks and often inspire each other. In 2026 they were spotted on a beach vacation together and on a Disney World trip.

Gigi is also known for a tight circle of friends, including Kendall Jenner, who is described as a longtime friend from their teens, Taylor Swift, Zoë Kravitz and Hailey Bieber. In July 2026 she was photographed at the rehearsal event for Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce's wedding.
