About Us

We research first.
Everything else is second.

Biography and Net Worth is a growing archive of the models, creators and personalities shaping culture right now — built on verified facts, not recycled gossip.

Why We Exist
The internet is full of biography pages copying each other's mistakes. We started this site to do the opposite — cross-check every date, every figure, every claim — before it ever reaches a reader.

What we stand for.

01

Verified, not viral

We don't chase trending rumors. Every biography goes through multiple public sources before it's published.

02

Updated, not frozen

Careers change fast. We revisit and update our profiles as new, verifiable information comes in.

03

Honest about uncertainty

Where a figure — like net worth — is an estimate, we say so clearly instead of presenting a guess as fact.

How a biography gets made.

Nothing goes live until it has been through the same four-step process, regardless of how well-known the subject is.

This is what separates a Biography and Net Worth page from a copy-paste template.

01

Source gathering

We collect public interviews, verified social accounts, press coverage and official records.

02

Cross-verification

Every fact is checked against at least two independent sources before it's accepted.

03

Editorial writing

The verified facts are written into a clear, readable profile — no filler, no clickbait.

04

Ongoing review

Pages are revisited as careers, relationships and public facts evolve.
