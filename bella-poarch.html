01 · Early Life

Early Life & Hometown

Bella Poarch was born on February 9, 1997, in San Fabian, Pangasinan, in the Philippines. Wikipedia lists her legal name as Denarie Bautista Taylor and says she was born Belinda Marie Macadengdeng Batumbakal before she was adopted. She was raised by her grandmother until about the age of three, in a very poor neighborhood, and was then adopted by a US military veteran and his Filipino wife. According to Rolling Stone, she joined two sisters and a brother, who were also adopted.

Bella Poarch Family: Adoption and a Move to America

When she was 13, her family, apart from her sisters who stayed in the Philippines, moved to San Francisco to stay with an aunt for several months. They then settled in Texas because her father needed bypass surgery. She has spoken openly about a hard childhood, including on the H3 Podcast in 2021, and has said she was bullied as a child, which later inspired her first single.

A girl raised by her grandmother in the Philippines became a US Navy sailor, then the owner of TikTok's most-liked video.

Why "Poarch"? And is it Bella Porches?

Poarch is pronounced like the word "porch", which is why many people search for Bella Porches, Bella Porch or just Poarch. It is not the surname she was born with. Her married name came from her ex-husband, Tyler Poarch, and she kept it as her professional name.

02 · TikTok

Bella Poarch TikTok: Rise to Fame

Bella Poarch created her TikTok account in January 2020 and began posting regularly in April 2020, mostly gaming and cosplay content. Her big moment came in August 2020, and within about a year she was one of the most-followed people on the app.

Bella on TikTok: the "M to the B" video

On August 17, 2020, she posted a close-up lip-sync to "M to the B" by British rapper Millie B. In the clip she bounces her head to the beat, with a blank stare and sharp expressions. It became the most-liked video on TikTok, and TikTok named it the biggest viral video of 2020. This one tik tok Bella Poarch posted is still the clip most people mean when they search for her.

Bella Poarch TikTok today

As of 2026 she has roughly 92 million followers on TikTok (@bellapoarch). Wikipedia describes her as one of the most-followed individuals on the platform and the most-followed person from the Philippines. She posts music teasers, cosplay and fashion more than lip-syncs now. Fans often search for her as "Bella on Tik Tok", "Bella Poarch Tik Tok" or "Tik Tok Bella Poarch", but the app is officially spelled TikTok.

Gaming, merch and the alpaca

In December 2020 she was reported to have drawn interest from the esports organizations 100 Thieves and FaZe Clan, as she also streamed on Twitch. Her alpaca stuffed toy, "Paca", became part of her image, and in 2020 she released a limited RIPNDIP x Paca clothing collection.

03 · Military

Bella Poarch Navy Service

Before TikTok, Bella Poarch served in the United States Navy. Wikipedia says she enlisted in 2017 as an aviation ordnanceman and served for three years, with postings in Japan and Hawaii. Some outlets give an earlier enlistment year and four years of service, and her label bio says she worked on helicopters and jets across the US, so the exact dates vary by source.

From Bella Poarch Navy to creator

She left the service in 2020 and started TikTok in January that year. Her Navy past is a big part of her story, and people often ask whether Bella Poarch really was in the military. The answer is yes, and she has spoken about it in interviews, including a Rolling Stone cover story in 2022 that framed her as someone who "conquered" her past, the Navy and TikTok.

04 · Career

Career & Music Timeline

Bella Poarch's career runs from viral video to pop music. After signing with Warner Records in 2021 she built a dark-pop sound, and in 2026 she went independent with her own label.

2020

"M to the B" goes viral

Starts TikTok in January, posts the most-liked video in August and signs a merch collection with RIPNDIP.

2021

Warner Records and "Build a Bitch"

Signs with Warner in May and releases her debut single on May 14, which peaks at number 56 on the Billboard Hot 100 and goes platinum in the US. "Inferno" with Sub Urban follows in August.

2022

Dolls EP

Releases the Dolls EP on August 12, with Grimes on "No Man's Land", and joins the Forbes 30 Under 30 list. Files for divorce in November.

2023

"Crush" and "Bad Boy"

Releases "Crush" with Lauv on September 15, "Bad Boy" on November 10 and wins a Kids' Choice Award. Her divorce is finalized in October.

2024

Collaborates with 6arelyhuman on "Don't Like Anybody", appears on Kenia Os' "F*CK OFF" and releases "Sweet Delusion" on October 18.

2025

"Will You Always Love Her"

Releases the single on March 21 and appears on a Vogue Philippines cover story in October.

2026

Independent era

Releases "Ribcage" on May 1, "My Boy" on June 26 and her debut album Picnic at the Cemetery on September 18 through Last Gang and her own label.

Music: from "Build a Bitch" to Picnic at the Cemetery

Her first single, made with producer Sub Urban and a video featuring Valkyrae, Mia Khalifa, Bretman Rock and ZHC, was about bullying and confidence. Rolling Stone praised the dark-pop tone of the Dolls EP. Picnic at the Cemetery is a cinematic coming-of-age album about reclaiming memory and healing after trauma, told through her hand-drawn characters called The Bigheads. She co-produced it, and she says the title comes from a Filipino tradition of gathering in the cemetery on November 1.

Awards

She won a Streamy Award for Breakout Creator in 2021, the iHeartRadio Social Star Award in 2022 and the Kids' Choice Award for Favorite Social Music Star in 2023 and 2024. "Build a Bitch" earned an MTV VMA nomination for Best Visual Effects.

06 · Personal Life

Marriage & Personal Life

Bella Poarch keeps her love life private. Her best-known relationship was her marriage to Tyler Poarch, and as of October 2026 she has not confirmed a new partner.

Tyler Poarch

She married Tyler Poarch in 2019, and the marriage stayed secret until reports in November 2022 that she had filed for divorce. The divorce was finalized in October 2023. On the Call Her Daddy podcast she said the split was mutual, that she and Tyler remained close and that her rise to fame strained the relationship. She also said that rumors of cheating, including the Tyga story, were not the reason.

07 · Tyga

Bella Poarch & Tyga: What Happened

The Bella Poarch Tyga story is the rumor that follows her most. Here is what is documented, and what is not.

The timeline

In September 2020 she appeared in TikTok videos with rapper Tyga, filmed at what looked like his Los Angeles home, and fans began to guess they were dating. In October 2020 an alleged intimate video of two people spread on Twitter with their names attached. There was never any evidence that the people in it were Bella Poarch or Tyga, and neither addressed it at the time.

Her response

On January 15, 2021 she posted a "2 Truths and a Lie" TikTok. It listed that Griffin Johnson slid into her DMs, that she made a tape with Tyga and that she was adopted, and she marked the Tyga line as the lie. She later said she received death threats over the rumors and that they were not why her marriage ended.

Fact Check

09 · Public Attention

Other Controversies

Bella Poarch has faced criticism since her first year of fame, but most of it has faded as her music career grew.

The tattoo apology

In September 2020 Korean social media users criticized her for a tattoo that resembled the Rising Sun Flag, a symbol linked to Japanese imperialism. She apologized, covered the tattoo and later had it removed.

Online hate and death threats

She has said the Tyga rumors brought a wave of hate and death threats, and she has spoken about how internet fame hurt her marriage. Some critics also called her "famous for nothing" in her early days, a view that has faded as her music was praised.

10 · Interviews

Interviews & What She Has Said

In her 2026 PAPER cover interview she described the new album as a storybook and said, "It's a very personal album." She also explained that the title refers to the Filipino tradition of a family picnic at the cemetery on November 1.
