01 · Early Life

Early Life & Family

Hailey Rhode Baldwin was born on November 22, 1996, in Tucson, Arizona. She was named after Halley's Comet, though her name is spelled differently. Her father is actor Stephen Baldwin, the youngest of the famous Baldwin brothers, and her mother is Kennya Deodato Baldwin, a Brazilian graphic designer of Italian and Portuguese descent.

Her maternal grandfather is the celebrated Brazilian musician Eumir Deodato, and her paternal uncle is actor Alec Baldwin, making the Baldwin acting dynasty part of her extended family. Her cousin is model and musician Ireland Baldwin. Before her marriage, she was known professionally and online simply as Hailey Baldwin, and fans still search for her under variations like Baldwin Hailey or her full maiden name, Hailey Rhode Baldwin.

Growing up around Hollywood and the fashion industry, she started modeling in her early teens.

Bieber grew up largely in the entertainment world, splitting time between New York and Los Angeles as her father's acting career took the family around the country. She has said in interviews that she was drawn to fashion and modeling from a young age, in part because of the creative circles her family moved in.

She signed her first modeling contract with New York agency Ford Models as a young teenager, later moving to IMG Models, and began appearing in magazines including Tatler, LOVE, V and i-D before landing her first major commercial campaign for French Connection in the winter of 2014.

It was also through her father, Stephen Baldwin, that Hailey first met Justin Bieber — she asked him to help her get the singer's autograph backstage at The Today Show in 2009, an encounter that would eventually grow into one of pop culture's most talked-about marriages.

02 · Career

Career Timeline

Bieber built a substantial runway and campaign career over the 2010s, walking for major fashion houses including Versace, Tommy Hilfiger, Dolce & Gabbana and Guess, and appearing on the covers of Vogue, Elle and other leading fashion magazines.

In June 2022, she launched Rhode, a minimalist skincare brand named after her own middle name, built around a small, curated lineup of products. What began as a direct-to-consumer beauty line grew rapidly, fueled by her personal following and a series of viral product launches, including a phone case that doubled as a lip-tint holder.

2025

Rhode sold to e.l.f. Beauty

e.l.f. Beauty agrees to acquire Rhode in a deal worth up to $1 billion in May, with Bieber staying on as Chief Creative Officer and Head of Innovation.

By the time of the acquisition, Rhode had grown from zero to $212 million in net sales in under three years, selling exclusively direct-to-consumer with just ten products — a growth rate that e.l.f. Beauty's CEO Tarang Amin called unlike anything he had seen in over three decades in the consumer industry.

The deal, announced in May 2025, consisted of $600 million in cash, $200 million in e.l.f. Beauty stock, and up to $200 million in additional earnout payments tied to Rhode's performance through 2028. As part of the agreement, Rhode began an in-store partnership with Sephora across North America and the UK by the end of 2025, and the brand has since expanded into European and Asian retail.

03 · Personal Life

Personal Life & Social Media

Hailey and Justin Bieber first met in 2009 and briefly dated between 2015 and 2016 before going their separate ways. They reconnected in the spring of 2018 after spending time together at a church conference, and Justin proposed during a trip to the Bahamas that July.

The couple legally married at a private New York courthouse ceremony on September 13, 2018, before holding a larger celebration for family and friends at the Montage Palmetto Bluff in South Carolina on September 30, 2019, with guests including Kendall Jenner, Kylie Jenner and Usher. In August 2024, the couple welcomed their first child, a son named Jack Blues Bieber.

Online, Bieber is among the most followed women in the world, with her Instagram account (@haileybieber) commanding tens of millions of followers. She is also active on TikTok, where she regularly shares skincare routines, fashion moments and glimpses of her family life.

Bieber has spoken candidly in interviews about the pressures of fame, a 2022 health scare involving a small blood clot and stroke-like symptoms, and the realities of balancing motherhood with running a major beauty company.

04 · Public Attention

Rumors & Public Attention

Bieber's marriage has occasionally drawn public scrutiny, particularly around her relationship with Justin's ex-girlfriend Selena Gomez's fanbase, with online speculation and criticism surfacing periodically since the couple's engagement in 2018.

In 2022, Bieber was hospitalized after experiencing stroke-like symptoms caused by a small blood clot in her brain, which she later attributed to a possible connection with hormonal birth control and a heart condition called PFO. She has since spoken openly about the experience to raise awareness.

The scale of the Rhode-to-e.l.f. deal also drew commentary from critics questioning how much of the valuation reflected celebrity influence versus product performance, a debate common to celebrity-founded beauty brands.
