01 · Early Life

Early Life & Family

Serena Page was born on September 19, 1999, in Houston, Texas, and grew up in the Houston area, including the suburb of Missouri City. She has two siblings, a brother and a sister, and she keeps their names and her parents' details private.

At Elkins High School she was a cheerleader for more than ten years, and tumbling was her favorite part. That long stretch of team sport is often linked to the confidence fans later saw on screen.

A quiet corporate career in marketing became one of reality TV's biggest breakout stories.

She enrolled at the University of Texas at San Antonio in 2018, joined the Alpha Kappa Alpha sorority and graduated in 2022 with a marketing degree. She also appeared in a 2023 short film called Battle.

After college she moved to Los Angeles and worked as a media planner at Moonbug Entertainment, the company behind children's YouTube brands. Viewers of Love Island USA met her as exactly that: a Houston girl with a day job and a big personality.

02 · Career

Career Timeline

Page's career is a textbook case of a reality show turning into a creator business. She started the summer of 2024 with roughly 2,000 Instagram followers and, according to TIME, finished it with millions.

Since then she has moved into beauty, fashion and lifestyle campaigns, signed with a major talent agency and picked up a place on one of the internet's most watched influencer lists.

03 · Love Story

Love Story: Serena & Kordell

On Day 1 Serena coupled up with Kordell Beckham, the younger brother of NFL star Odell Beckham Jr. The pair were early favorites, and their slow-burn romance became the emotional spine of the season.

Nothing was smooth, though. Serena stepped away from the pairing on Day 13 during the Stick or Twist game, went on a boat date with Nigel, then re-coupled with Kordell on Day 15. She competed in challenges like Vacation Vixens and the Fourth of July task, and she won both.

In January 2026 she said on Kamie Crawford's podcast that marriage is in the talks and will happen when Kordell decides to propose. In August 2026 she told E! Serena, Leah Kateb and JaNa Craig also formed the "PPG" (Powerpuff Girls) friendship that fans followed long after the villa.

04 · Public Attention

Controversies & Drama

The biggest drama of Serena's story was on screen. During Casa Amor, she received video footage of Kordell getting close to Daia McGhee. At the next recoupling she refused to pick anyone, Kordell returned with Daia, and the fallout led to the much-discussed dock argument. Viewers praised her for standing up for herself, and many said it was rare to see a Black woman shown that way on a dating show.

Online, she also faced think pieces that claimed she did not care about Kordell because she did not chase him. In a Teen Vogue interview she brushed this off. In October 2024 she also asked people on X to stop tagging her in negative posts about her friends.

Context Matters

Show drama is not scandal.

As of September 2026, we found no verified reports of legal trouble or off-screen scandal involving Serena Page. Most of the controversy is fan debate about Casa Amor and relationship timelines.

05 · Interviews

Interviews & What She Has Said

In a HuffPost profile from December 2024, Page said the attention can feel overwhelming and that she gets overstimulated easily, but added that she has "pretty tough skin" when it comes to online comments. She also admitted that in past relationships she was more immature and reacted strongly when let down.

Speaking to Teen Vogue in July 2025, she said there was no shift between the villa and real life, and that they were still in the "honeymoon phase."

In a 2026 podcast chat with fellow Season 6 star Kaylor Martin, she said she was traveling, working with brands and preparing a major project she hopes to announce before the end of the year. She also spoke at Northwestern University's State of the Black Union event in May 2026 about her time in the villa and working in entertainment as a Black woman.

06 · More

Net Worth & 2026 Highlights

Serena Page has no verified net worth. One public estimate puts it at about $250,000 to $750,000, built from her $50,000 share of the prize, brand campaigns, appearances and social media. These are unofficial figures, and her growing brand list may push real earnings higher.

In 2026 she attended Billboard Women in Music in April, spoke at Northwestern in May, was named to TIME100 Creators in July and appeared at Variety's Power of Young Hollywood in August. She turned 27 on September 19, 2026.
