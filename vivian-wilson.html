01 · Early Life

Early Life & Family

Vivian Jenna Wilson was born on April 17, 2004, in Los Angeles, California, the eldest surviving child of Tesla and SpaceX founder Elon Musk and his first wife, Canadian author Justine Wilson. She has a twin brother, Griffin, and three younger triplet brothers, Kai, Saxon and Damian, all conceived via IVF during their parents' marriage, which lasted from 2000 to 2008.

Through her mother, Wilson holds Canadian citizenship in addition to her American citizenship. She attended Crossroads School, a private school in Santa Monica, and has described her childhood as difficult, citing her father's frequent absences and what she has called a cold and uncaring dynamic at home.

A quiet Instagram story in 2020 was the first step toward living publicly as herself.

Wilson has said she first came out as transgender to an aunt by text message at age 16, asking her to keep it from her father for the time being. She came out more widely via a public Instagram story in 2020 and began treatment for gender dysphoria shortly after.

She has said that beginning gender-affirming treatment as a minor required her father's permission, and that her relationship with him grew more distant through her teenage years as her identity became a point of tension within the family. Her mother, by contrast, has been a consistent and vocal source of support.

On April 18, 2022 — one day after turning 18 — Wilson filed a petition in Los Angeles County Superior Court to legally change her name from Xavier Alexander Musk to Vivian Jenna Wilson, adopting her mother's maiden name. Her filing cited both her gender identity and, in her words, that she no longer wished to be related to her biological father "in any way, shape, or form." The petition was granted later that year.

Vivian and Griffin Musk grew up as the eldest of Elon and Justine's children, ahead of triplet brothers Kai, Saxon and Damian. Unlike Vivian, Griffin has kept a low public profile and has not spoken extensively in the press about their shared childhood or their father. Coverage of "Elon Musk's daughter Vivian Wilson" frequently references Griffin as her twin, but he has largely stayed outside the spotlight that has followed his sister since 2022.

02 · Career

Career Timeline

After high school, Wilson enrolled at Temple University's Japan campus before deciding to leave and return to Los Angeles, where she began pursuing modeling and community college studies in language. Her public profile grew steadily through 2025, built on candid interviews rather than a traditional launch.

In March 2025, she appeared on the cover of Teen Vogue in a profile that covered her upbringing, her transition and her relationship with her father in her own words. Two months later, she made her modeling debut in a campaign for the inclusive fashion label Wildfang, co-designing a T-shirt with proceeds benefiting The Trevor Project, an organization focused on suicide prevention for LGBTQ+ youth.

Her runway work has since expanded to include collaborations with designers and brands such as Prabal Gurung and Dauphinette, alongside inclusive labels like TomBoyX and Boy Smells. She has also appeared at LGBTQ+-focused cultural events, including a runway walk connected to RuPaul's Drag Race.

Alongside modeling, Wilson has become a recurring speaker on LGBTQ+ issues, appearing at the 2025 Teen Vogue Summit in Los Angeles, where she pushed back publicly on online narratives linking transgender people to violence and spoke about the impact of anti-trans legislation.

03 · Personal Life

Personal Life & Social Media

Wilson has said she lives with roommates in Los Angeles, pays her own rent, and attends community college while balancing modeling work and part-time jobs. She has been explicit in interviews that she does not receive financial support from her father and has pushed back on rumors that she has access to a large trust fund.

She has described her relationship with her mother, Justine Wilson, as close and supportive, while her relationship with her father remains, by her own account, non-existent; she has said the two have not spoken in years. She has also spoken about her sexuality, describing herself as bisexual.

Online, Wilson is active on Instagram and TikTok under the handle @vivllainous, where she posts about fashion, her modeling work and LGBTQ+ advocacy, alongside more personal, often candid commentary that outlets have described as "chronically online."

She has used her platform consistently to discuss youth mental health and gender-affirming care access, often tying her personal story to broader policy debates affecting transgender people in the United States.

04 · Public Attention

Estrangement & Public Attention

Wilson's public profile is closely tied to her estrangement from her father. In interviews with outlets including NBC News and Teen Vogue, she has described Elon Musk as "cold," "quick to anger," "uncaring" and "narcissistic," and has referred to him publicly as a "pathetic man-child."

Wilson has rejected suggestions that her existence or transition influenced her father's political shift, saying in interviews that the idea is not accurate. She has also said she does not expect to inherit any part of his fortune and has no desire to be "super-rich."

She has faced targeted harassment online tied to her father's public profile and to anti-transgender rhetoric, which she has addressed directly in public appearances, including her 2025 remarks rejecting the claim that transgender people are disproportionately linked to violence.

05 · More

Net Worth & Recent Highlights

Vivian Wilson's net worth has not been independently verified, and she has said publicly that she does not have significant personal wealth, describing her income as coming from modeling work, brand collaborations, speaking engagements and part-time jobs. Rumors of a multibillion-dollar trust fund tied to her father's fortune have circulated online but have been directly denied by Wilson herself.

In 2025 and 2026 she expanded her modeling career considerably, signing with CAA, walking in New York Fashion Week, and fronting the Desigual "Born to Disobey" campaign in September 2026, while continuing to speak publicly on LGBTQ+ issues.
